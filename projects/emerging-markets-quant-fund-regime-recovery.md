# Emerging-Markets Quant Fund — Regime Recovery & Client Transparency

> **Portfolio lens:** Portfolio monitoring, regime risk, downside control, allocator transparency, and AUM retention.

## Executive snapshot

Led a coordinated investment and client response when a $5B emerging-markets strategy failed under a new volatility and liquidity regime. Circuit breakers, regime-specific specialists, walk-forward validation, behavioral churn signals, and a transparent risk portal repaired both portfolio adaptability and allocator trust.

## Resume-ready impact

- Led crisis response for a $5B fund, containing risk before rebuilding the strategy around explicit market regimes and bounded capital deployment.
- Detected and corrected lookahead bias before live scale, then piloted the new model on a $500M Brazil-and-India portfolio.
- Achieved 99.8% retention, protected $225M of identified at-risk assets, added $200M through referrals, and raised NPS from 78 to 96.

## Interview story

### Situation

A mean-reversion model kept buying declines after inflation and geopolitical shocks shifted markets into a persistent trend regime. Slippage increased tenfold, while delayed black-box reporting left clients unable to understand risk.

### Task

Protect capital, validate a new strategy without leakage, and prevent silent allocator attrition while preserving proprietary investment logic.

### Actions

- Introduced immediate circuit breakers and manual risk reduction before model redesign.
- Replaced one monolithic strategy with regime detection and specialist modes for normal, volatile, and crisis conditions.
- Red-teamed the data pipeline, removed future-information leakage, and adopted walk-forward validation.
- Built a client portal showing regime, risk drivers, specialist posture, liquidity, and guarded scenario analysis.

### Results

- Retention reached 99.8%.
- The program protected $225M of $500M in identified at-risk AUM.
- Referrals added $200M in new AUM, and NPS reached 96.
- The fund reported 14.8% performance versus a 9.33% benchmark after recovery.

## Decisions and trade-offs

- Contain exposure before attempting to optimize the model.
- Treat the investment strategy and the client's understanding of risk as coupled products.
- Use guided transparency without disclosing proprietary source code.

## Leadership signal

Aligned portfolio management, quant research, risk, execution, investor relations, and allocators around explicit recovery evidence and remaining uncertainty.
