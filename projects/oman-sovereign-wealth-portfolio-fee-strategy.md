# Oman Sovereign Wealth Mandate — Portfolio & Fee Strategy

> **Portfolio lens:** Portfolio construction, sovereign allocator strategy, risk budgeting, capability transfer, and fee design.

## Executive snapshot

Reframed a sovereign mandate from a false choice between high-return/high-drawdown growth and over-conservative capital preservation. An 80/20 core-satellite portfolio, explicit risk budget, phased allocation, in-country capability building, and hurdle-based fees aligned national objectives with investment mechanics.

## Resume-ready impact

- Led proposal design for a $1B sovereign mandate by converting internal disagreement into an explicit total-fund risk budget and one client narrative.
- Structured an 80% capital-preservation core and 20% growth satellite with exposure caps, quarterly rebalancing, live risk monitoring, and circuit breakers.
- Secured an initial $500M allocation that expanded to $1.2B within 12 months and generated $15M in first-year fees.

## Interview story

### Situation

Quant teams favored an emerging-markets strategy targeting 12–15% returns with drawdowns above 25%, while Sales preferred a Treasury-heavy 6–8% portfolio. Neither matched the client's combined preservation, diversification, and capability goals.

### Task

Design an investable whole-portfolio proposition that balanced intergenerational capital, growth, liquidity, governance, local value, and aligned commercial terms.

### Actions

- Paused the pitch and ran a two-day workshop across Quant, Sales, Compliance, and relationship teams.
- Defined one sovereign objective set and allocated risk rather than political influence to portfolio sleeves.
- Embedded phased deployment, exposure caps, circuit breakers, rebalancing, and investment-committee review.
- Added a six-month talent-rotation concept and aligned base versus performance fees to the different jobs of core and satellite capital.

### Results

- The initial allocation was $500M.
- Total allocation expanded to $1.2B within 12 months.
- First-year fees reached $15M.
- The proposition generated invitations to pitch approximately $2B in additional regional mandates.

## Decisions and trade-offs

- Resolve the false binary through core-satellite construction.
- Make governance and capability transfer part of the investment product.
- Use phased commitment so trust and capital grow through evidence.

## Leadership signal

Unified internal investment and commercial teams around the allocator's national objectives, then translated the strategy into portfolio, governance, talent, and fee mechanisms.
