# Trading Algorithm Recovery — Regime-Aware Turnaround

> **Portfolio lens:** Portfolio turnaround, risk-adjusted performance, gated capital, cross-functional investment governance, and AUM growth.

## Executive snapshot

Took ownership of a $5B emerging-markets trading strategy after a three-month decline caused a $150M loss. A single crisis team separated signal and execution failure, redesigned the strategy for regime awareness, imposed deterministic risk boundaries, and earned capital back through a $500M gated pilot.

## Resume-ready impact

- Led recovery of a $5B algorithmic strategy after a $150M loss, unifying Quant, Trading, Risk, Compliance, Data Science, and Investor Relations.
- Redesigned the system around regime classification, ensemble selection, adaptive exposure, stress testing, and deterministic capital limits.
- Improved pilot ROI 12%, reduced slippage 40%, restored Sharpe from 0.6 to 1.2, recovered the loss within nine months, and attracted $200M in new AUM.

## Interview story

### Situation

The model was trained on a decade of stable history, missed a new volatility and liquidity regime, and compounded losses because monthly reviews and functional silos delayed escalation.

### Task

Contain further downside, prove a technically and operationally credible redesign, restore client trust, and scale only after measurable investment gates.

### Actions

- Reconstructed trade-level performance using FIX logs, market data, slippage, macro events, and model decisions.
- Separated signal error from execution error and replayed the failing periods.
- Stress-tested currency crises, flash crashes, sanctions, elections, and liquidity shocks.
- Deployed first to Brazil and India with weekly reviews and capital tranches tied to ROI, Sharpe, drawdown, and slippage.

### Results

- Pilot ROI improved 12% within three months.
- Execution slippage declined 40%, and Sharpe improved from 0.6 to 1.2.
- The $150M loss was recovered within nine months.
- The strategy added an estimated $40M in annualized profit and attracted $200M in new AUM.

## Decisions and trade-offs

- Treat the decline as a system failure, not a bad quarter.
- Keep adaptive models inside non-negotiable capital and drawdown limits.
- Restore capital in tranches rather than through an immediate full-fund relaunch.

## Leadership signal

Created one crisis charter and evidence model across investment, engineering, risk, compliance, execution, and client functions, then institutionalized 24/7 monitoring and a reusable playbook.
